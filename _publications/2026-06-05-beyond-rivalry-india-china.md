This policy brief introduces the D.E.A.L. Strategic Choice Matrix, an original game-theoretic framework. It examines four strategic pathways for India-China relations, ultimately arguing for a model of "Functional Strategic Coexistence" built on institutionalized restraint and selective cooperation[cite: 1].
